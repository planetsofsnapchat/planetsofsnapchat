What are Snapchat Planets?

Snapchat Planets are a visual part of Friend Solar Systems. You are shown as the Sun, while a friend appears in one of eight planet positions connected to the Best Friends ranking.

01

A visual position, not a personality test

The planet tells you the displayed position in the feature. It does not measure someone's feelings or explain an entire friendship.

08

Eight positions

The order runs from Mercury to Neptune. Each name represents a position, not a scientific meaning.

Understand the system

How does it work?

Think of it as a small map around your profile: the Sun is you, and the planets show positions in the displayed Best Friends system.

1

Friend Solar Systems are associated with Snapchat+ and may depend on account access and app availability.

Two connected ideas

Best Friends vs Friend Solar Systems

They work together, but each one answers a different question about your Snapchat connections.

Best Friends

Best Friends are the people you interact with most often. The list is based on activity patterns and may change as your conversations, snaps and chats change.

  • Shows your closest Snapchat connections
  • Can change as interaction changes
  • Does not measure feelings or friendship value
  • May include people who are not in the same planet position

Friend Solar Systems

Friend Solar Systems turn a displayed Best Friends position into a visual planet map. You are the Sun, and a friend appears in one of eight positions around you.

  • Uses Mercury through Neptune
  • Explains a position visually
  • Is symbolic rather than scientific
  • Does not reveal private messages or hidden information

Key takeaway

A planet is a simple in-app label, not a complete score of your friendship. Use it as a fun way to understand the feature rather than as proof of how someone feels.

Troubleshooting

Common issues and fixes

Start with these simple checks before assuming your account has a problem.

My planet is missing

Check Snapchat+ access, update the app and reopen it. Availability can differ by account or region.

My planet changed

The displayed ranking may have changed. A new position is not automatically a negative sign.

The menu looks different

Snapchat changes its interface. Look in the current Snapchat+ or profile settings.

A friend cannot see it

Both accounts may not have the same feature access or settings.

The ranking feels wrong

The app's ranking may not match your personal expectations or the way you describe a friendship.

A website asks for your password

Do not share your password with third-party sites claiming to unlock or repair Snapchat features.

Frequently asked

Quick answers

Short answers to the questions visitors usually have after seeing a planet.

Which planet is first?

Mercury is the first position in the eight-planet order.

Which planet is eighth?

Neptune is the eighth and final position in the displayed order.

Can my Snapchat planet change?

Yes. The displayed position can change when the relevant ranking changes or the feature is displayed differently.

Are Snapchat Planets real astronomy?

No. The feature uses planet names and visuals symbolically. It is not a scientific model of friendship.

Do planets prove who my best friend is?

They show an in-app position, but they cannot fully describe a friendship or someone's feelings.

Why can I not find Friend Solar Systems?

Check whether Snapchat+ is available for your account, update Snapchat and review the current profile or Snapchat+ settings.

Can I disable Snapchat Planets?

Look for the Friend Solar Systems or Snapchat+ controls in your current app settings. The exact menu can change between versions.

Is this website official Snapchat support?

No. This is an independent informational guide. For account-specific help, use Snapchat's official support channels.

The big picture

Conclusion

Snapchat Planets are easiest to understand when you treat them as a visual guide—not a judgment.

Keep it simple

Mercury is the first position and Neptune is the eighth. Your displayed planet can change, and that change does not automatically mean anything negative. Explore the feature, check the current Snapchat settings when needed, and remember that real friendships are more meaningful than an app label.
