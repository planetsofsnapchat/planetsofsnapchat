Best Friends

Understand the difference between Best Friends, rankings and Friend Solar Systems.

What does Best Friends mean?

Best Friends are people you interact with frequently on Snapchat. The displayed ranking can change over time as your activity changes.

How does it connect to planets?

Friend Solar Systems use planet positions to show a friend's place in the displayed ranking. Mercury is the first position and Neptune is the eighth.

Why did my friend's planet change?

A change may simply mean the displayed ranking changed. It is not reliable evidence of someone's feelings, loyalty or the value of your friendship.

What should you remember?

  • App rankings are not a complete picture of real relationships.
  • A planet is a visual label, not a personal judgment.
  • Feature access and menus can change with Snapchat updates.
  • Use official Snapchat settings for account-specific controls.
Enjoy the feature as a visual guide, but avoid using a planet position to judge yourself or someone else.
