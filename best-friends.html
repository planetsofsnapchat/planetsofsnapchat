Snapchat Best Friends, badges, and what they mean

The Best Friends list is the foundation everything else is built on, badges included. This page walks through how the list is built, what each badge tells you, and how it feeds into your planets.

What is the Best Friends list?

An automatically generated ranking, not a manual one.

Snapchat keeps a running list of the people you interact with most, based on snaps sent and received, chat activity, and streaks. That list isn't public in one place for anyone to browse; it shows up indirectly, through badges on friends' profiles and, for Snapchat+ subscribers, through planets.

You don't add or remove anyone from it directly. It updates on its own as your messaging habits change, which is why a friendship that goes quiet for a while can quietly slip down the ranking without any action on your part.

Best Friend Badge vs. Friend Badge: what is the difference?

Both badges show up on a profile, but they mean different things.

BadgeWhat triggers itWhat it tells you
Best Friends badge You appear in their top eight, and they appear in yours. The closeness is mutual on both sides, which is also the requirement for a shared planet to appear.
Friends badge You appear in their top eight, but they don't currently appear in yours (or the reverse). Interaction is happening, but it's more one-sided, at least based on current activity.

Why the direction matters

Snapchat calculates each person's top eight independently. It's entirely possible to be someone's #1 while they only rank fourth or fifth on your own list.

Badges change quietly

A badge can shift from Best Friends to Friends, or disappear altogether, without any notification. It simply reflects the latest activity snapshot.

Badges unlock planets

A mutual Best Friends badge is what allows a shared planet to show up for Snapchat+ users with the feature turned on.

Who can actually see your ranking

The list stays more private than people often assume.

No public list

Snapchat never publishes a readable Best Friends list anywhere in the app for others to browse.

Badge only

What a friend sees is limited to a Best Friends or Friends badge on your shared profile view.

Planet is mutual-only

A shared planet needs both accounts on Snapchat+ with the feature switched on; one-sided setups show nothing.

How the ranking actually works

The same three signals show up again and again.

  • Volume: the total number of snaps and chats exchanged over a recent stretch of time.
  • Streaks: an ongoing streak is treated as a strong, consistent signal of contact.
  • Recency: more recent activity carries more weight than older activity, so the list can shift even without a big change in overall volume.

None of this is visible as a raw score inside the app. What you see are the outcomes: a badge, a position in someone's solar system, or an emoji next to a friend's name in your chat list.

It's also worth remembering the list is per-friendship, not a single global scoreboard. You don't have one universal "best friend"; you have a separate top eight calculated for every person who has you in theirs, and each of those rankings moves independently based on your activity with that one person.

Common questions about best friends and planets

Why does my best friend see a different badge for me than I see for them?

Because the two rankings are calculated separately. If you interact with them more than they interact with you, you can hold a Best Friends badge on their profile while they only show a Friends badge on yours.

Can I hide my Best Friends list from others?

You can't publish or hide a list directly, since Snapchat never displays it as a single page. What people can see is limited to badges on your profile and, if you both use Snapchat+ with the feature enabled, a shared planet.

Does removing someone as a friend affect the ranking of others?

It can indirectly. If a top-ranked friend is removed, the friend just below them on your activity list can move up to fill the open spot.

Is the emoji next to a friend's name the same as a badge?

They're related but separate. Chat emojis are a longer-standing Snapchat feature that also reflects interaction levels, while the Best Friends and Friends badges appear on profiles and specifically gate the planets feature.
